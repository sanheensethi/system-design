How to Read This Book

HLD is best learned by deriving architectures, not memorizing them. Chapter 1 gives you the framework. Chapter 3 shows that framework applied to simple problems progressively. By Chapter 5, you'll see how the same levers scale to billion-user systems.

Interview Strategy
A 45-60 min HLD interview follows this script: 5 min requirements, 5 min capacity estimation, 5 min API design, 5 min data model, 10 min architecture diagram, 15-20 min deep dives on bottlenecks. Chapter 1 gives you this framework; Chapters 3-5 give you the practiced examples.
